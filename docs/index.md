# Introduction

Welcome — this is my notebook, a running collection of notes I take as I read and consume content. 

The wiki is meant to be read [in the web format](https://beyondgoodevil.github.io/memorabilia/).

## How it's organized

!!! info "Four sections"
    - **Book Notes** — Notes from my readings.
    - **Podcast/Video Notes** — Takeaways from podcast episodes and YouTube videos.
    - **Lecture Notes** — Notes from lectures I attend/watch.
    - **Misc Notes** — Random thoughts I am too lazy to organize.

Use the sidebar on the left to browse by section, or the search bar at the top to jump straight to a topic. Every note has its own table of contents on the right for quick navigation within long pages.
